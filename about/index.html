WHO I AM

Who is Wasim Dev?

My name is MD Wasimul Bari Shah, and I use Wasim Dev as my personal developer identity.

I study Law and Land Administration at the University of Rajshahi. Alongside my academic work, I have a strong interest in software development, web security and digital technology.

My interests include web and application development, penetration testing, bug bounty research, cybersecurity, digital governance and the relationship between law and technology.

The goal is to combine legal knowledge with practical technical skills and explore how secure, useful and responsible technology can contribute to modern society.
